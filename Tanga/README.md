# 📖 Project Summary

This project involved the installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance of modern solar street lighting systems designed to improve public safety, road visibility and energy efficiency.

Two different solar street lighting technologies were deployed during the project:

## ✅ All-in-One Solar Street Lights

Integrated solar street lighting system where the solar panel, lithium battery, LED luminaire and MPPT controller are built into a single compact unit.

### Features

- Integrated Solar Panel
- LiFePO₄ Battery
- MPPT Charge Controller
- High Efficiency LED
- Intelligent Lighting Modes
- Motion Sensor

---

## ✅ OPPO Solar Street Lights

High-performance solar street lighting system designed for highways, urban roads and public spaces.

### Features

- High Power LED Module
- LiFePO₄ Battery
- Intelligent MPPT Controller
- Remote Configuration
- Automatic Dusk-to-Dawn Operation
- Long Service Life
